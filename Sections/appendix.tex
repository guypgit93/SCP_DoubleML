\subsection{Background characteristics by pre-/post-SCP period}
\label{sec:appendix-a1-period}

Table~\ref{tab:summary_a1_by_period} decomposes the background characteristics reported in Table~\ref{tab:summary_background} (Section~\ref{sec:data}) by pre-/post-SCP period within each country (split at the November 2022 cutoff), replicating \citet{andersen2025}'s Table A1. The pre/post composition of each country's sample is broadly stable, with the exception of a rise in the disabled-household share in both countries and a fall in Scotland's household size in the post period.
